# Code of Conduct

Podu adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background or experience.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or private.
- No sexual language or imagery, and no unwelcome attention.
- Don't publish anyone's private information.

## Where it applies

In every Podu space: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any channel where you represent the project.

## Reporting

Email **hello@podu.dev** with what happened and any links. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone who breaks these rules, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
