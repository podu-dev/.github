# Getting help

- **Setting up your pod:** follow the host guides at [www.podu.dev](https://www.podu.dev) (Vercel, Netlify, GitHub Pages, Render, Cloudflare Pages, your own server).
- **Something not working:** open an issue in the [app](https://github.com/podu-dev/app) repo with your pod name, your host, and what you see. Never post secrets or tokens.
- **Questions:** email **hello@podu.dev**.
- **A pod breaking the rules:** email **abuse@podu.dev**.
- **A security issue:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
