# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please email **security@podu.dev**. Don't open a public issue for security problems.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it. We'll confirm we got your report within 3 working days and keep you updated until it's fixed.

Please give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before you share details publicly.

## What's in scope

- The Podu app and API at `www.podu.dev`
- How Podu creates, changes and removes DNS records for pods
- Sign-in, sessions and account limits

## Out of scope

- Sites hosted *on* a pod (`*.podu.dev`). Those belong to their owners. To report one that breaks the rules, email **abuse@podu.dev**.
- Denial-of-service tests, spam, and social engineering.

## Abuse vs security

A pod used for phishing, malware or impersonation is **abuse**, not a vulnerability. Report it to **abuse@podu.dev**. Obvious phishing is taken down within 24 hours.
